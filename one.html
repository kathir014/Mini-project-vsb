peaky blinders

Peaky Blinders is a hit TV show about a gangster family in England. It takes place after World War I. The boss is Tommy Shelby. He is smart, brave, and tough. His gang makes money through illegal betting. They are famous for their sharp suits and hats with hidden razor blades.The story starts in the city of Birmingham. Tommy wants his family to be rich and safe. His big plans often lead to deadly fights with rival gangs and politicians. Along the way, the family deals with secrets and deep troubles

Tommy Shelby's dangerous journey

Thomas "Tommy" Shelby, portrayed by Cillian Murphy, is the complex, chain-smoking, and sharp-suited leader of the Peaky Blinders. As a haunted World War I veteran from Birmingham, he transforms his family from a local razor gang into a massive criminal and business empire. Tommy is ruthless and clever, always thinking three steps ahead of his enemies.
